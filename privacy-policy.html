Privacy Policy — Screw Jam: Wood Pin Puzzle

Developer: Nirmal Games

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nirmal Games handles information when you use the mobile game Screw Jam: Wood Pin Puzzle ("the App").

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App does not require account registration, login, or personal profile submission, and Nirmal Games does not operate backend servers to store your personal information. Game progress, stars, coins, boosters, garage rank, unlocked themes, stats, and settings are saved locally on your device. You can erase this data anytime from Settings → Reset Data or Android App Storage settings.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The App displays Banner and optional Rewarded Video ads powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID via AD_ID / AdServices) and device/app-set identifiers
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address
  • App interactions & ad engagement (impressions, clicks, rewarded completions)
  • Device & technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, crash logs)

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Managing Ad Preferences & Opting Out

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

4. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.
